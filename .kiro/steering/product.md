# Product

Haunted Hollow is a Halloween site where the visitor walks into a haunted estate. Style: eerie, gothic, folklore. No gore or graphic violence.

Safety rules:

- Show a photosensitivity and sudden-sound warning before entry.
- Jump scares are off by default. Safe mode disables scares and flashing.
- Flashing never exceeds 3 times per second. Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
- No accounts, no backend, no uploads. User data stays in localStorage.
